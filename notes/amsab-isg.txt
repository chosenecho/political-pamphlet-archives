Savings by the Flemish government

When all the lights go out, what is left?

The Flemish government announced that subsidies for the national archives – including Amsab-ISG – will be halved starting in January 2027.

The ruthlessness, abruptness, and arbitrariness of that decision are barely comprehensible.

The cutbacks not only have a severe and direct impact on our operations and our team, but also undermine a crucial societal foundation. A society that treats its past so carelessly puts its future at stake as well.

Bread & Roses is cheating

For the 30th birthday Bread & Roses we invited friendly heritage institutions to share special pieces from their own collections that, in their opinion, resonate with the meaning of the legendary slogan behind the magazine's name:We want bread and we want roses too'.

Thanks to the enthusiastic cooperation of numerous organizations, the virtual expo brings Bread & Roses is cheating a polyphonic selection of objects, images, documents, and stories together.
